### Input page (DW Studio → Scene Input / Preview)
- **Library terrain filter:** a "Terrain" dropdown (Hilly, Coastal, Agricultural, Urban) on the right, under the
  collection chips. A choice with no tiles keeps the current results and shows a notice with an x, which closes itself
  after 4 s (`filterLibrary`, `noMatchMessage`, `applyFilter` in `src/input-view.js`).
- **Cold-start / loading messages** for the catalog, thumbnails and preview (`LOADING_COPY`, `COLD_AFTER_MS` = 2.5 s).
- **Upload without a geotransform:** a card asks for the GSD (m/pixel). START GENERATION waits for it (`isReady`). It is
  sent with `POST /api/input/{id}/gsd`, and the flat 3D plane gets the image's real size (see §2).
- **Box outlines:** a thin glow, green in dark mode and orange in bright mode (`--glow-rgb`). No moving patch.
- **Background** (`src/bg-float.js` maths, unit-tested; `src/bg-icons.js` driver; symbols in `index.html` from `icons/2.svg`):
  - 12 icons: 2 big, 3 medium, 7 small.
  - Each cruises at a steady speed (8 / 9.5 / 11 px/s) around its home spot and never halts.
  - They bounce off each other and off their limits.
  - They keep 1 cm apart; icons of the same artwork keep 5 cm apart.
  - The big satellite is locked to the top-left quadrant and the earth + satellite to the bottom-right.
  - No icon is ever more than 30% hidden behind a box or past an edge. The left limit is a fixed line at the collapsed
    sidebar's width (56 px).
  - Home spots are spread evenly on all four sides (`layoutHomes`) and recomputed on resize.
  - The very small icons are 1.4x on the left side.
  - The cursor pushes icons and clicks kick them; they ease back to cruise speed.
  - Star dots: half the earlier density; 6% are big.
  - Everything holds still under prefers-reduced-motion.

### Processing page and jobs
- **Jobs:** any job can be opened while another generates (`job.run`, `showRunningJob`). Each job keeps its viewer edits
  (`captureViewerMemory` / `applyViewerMemory`). A failed generation is marked **Failed** and gets
  **↻ RETRY GENERATION** (`syncStartButton`, `retryJob`). "N jobs running" counts only the generating job.
- **Box readouts** follow the real work, with a 1.3 s minimum each (`src/progress-sync.js` `trackWork`). The
  calculation log is queued and paced (280 ms per line) and finishes before the DEM Elevation box.
- **Captions:**
  - The depth box has 3 lines: model / inference / generation.
  - No tier or DEM names anywhere on the processing page.
  - The log gives footprint, relief, grid and mesh spacing (`generationLogLines`).

### 3D viewer (Studio)
- **Flat-terrain warning:** flat Sentinel-2 tiles get a blinking red "Low-relief scene" card with the tile's footprint
  (`src/flat-warning.js`).
- **Vertical exaggeration:** extreme highs and lows (Tukey 1.5 IQR) get a soft ceiling (`src/outlier-relief.js`, used
  in `src/terrain.js`).
- **Wireframe colour** follows the theme live (`terrain.refreshThemeColors`).

### Bright mode
- Every green is the site orange: `--neon-rgb` 196, 120, 58; background icons, stars and outlines 223, 118, 32.
  **Tier 2's green "T2" badges stay green** (a status colour: green = Tier 2, orange = Tier 1).
- Crisper greys: the text base is 40, 24, 10 (was 58, 42, 26), and the main surfaces have stronger outlines.

## 2. Backend (`backend/`)
- `api/depth_routes.py`: transient Space errors (502/503/504, ClientDisconnect, resets) are retried after 2 s and 5 s
  with a fresh gradio Client (`SPACE_RETRY_DELAYS_S`, `_is_transient`). Quota errors and timeouts are not retried.
- `dem/glo30.py`: `mosaic_to_grid` merges GLO-30 tiles, then reprojects once. The old per-tile reproject left NaNs on
  tile seams: Darjeeling's south edge is on 27°N, which gave 6 NaN pixels.
- `terrain/mesh_export.py`: `fill_nan_nearest` fills holes from neighbours, never the tile minimum, which drew 554 m
  "downward spikes".
- `input/store.py` + `api/input_routes.py`: `gsd_required` on uploads without a geotransform; `set_manual_gsd` /
  `POST /api/input/{id}/gsd` (0.01–1000 m/pixel; refused when the file has a geotransform).
- `generation/pipeline.py`: with a manual GSD, the flat plane is sized pixels × GSD (real metres for Measure).
